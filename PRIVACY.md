# pokebot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 07/11/2026

## 1. Who we are

pokebot (the "Bot") is a text-based Discord bot operated by mark ("we", "us"), based in England. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy should be read together with our [Terms of Service](README.md#terms-of-service).

## 2. Unofficial fan project

The Bot is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc., The Pokémon Company, or Discord Inc. Pokémon and all related names are trademarks of their respective owners. The Bot is free, non-commercial, and uses text only.

## 3. What we collect and store

We store only what is needed to run the Bot:

- **Your Discord user ID**, so we can keep your Pokédex separate from other players.
- **Your Pokédex**: which of the original 151 Pokémon you have caught, and how many of each.

While a wild Pokémon is waiting to be caught, the Bot briefly remembers the channel ID it appeared in. This is held in memory only and is lost when the Bot restarts.

We may also keep temporary logs for security and abuse prevention, for example to investigate attacks or misuse of the Bot.

## 4. What we do not collect

We do not store your messages. The Bot responds to its slash commands (`/pokemon`, `/pokeball` and `/pokedex`) and to the text commands `--pokemon`, `--pokeball` and `--pokedex`. To spot the text commands, the Bot has to read messages in the channels it can see, but it only checks whether a message starts with `--` and matches one of those commands. Every other message is ignored straight away and is never stored, logged, or shared. We do not collect your email address, location, payment details, or any other personal information through the Bot. Your Discord username is shown in the Bot's replies but is not stored.

## 5. How we use your data

We use your data only to run the Bot (keeping your Pokédex and showing it to you), to keep the Bot secure, and to deal with abuse as described in our Terms of Service. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

## 6. Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law, we process this data because it is necessary for our legitimate interests in running a free fan game and keeping it secure, and because it is needed to provide the features you choose to use. The data is minimal and low risk.

## 7. Sharing your data

We do not sell or rent your data. We may share information only:

- with Discord Inc. when reporting abuse or violations of its rules;
- with the police, courts, or other authorities where the law requires it or where we are dealing with conduct that may be unlawful, such as an attack on the Bot; and
- with our hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf.

Discord has its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) that covers how Discord handles your information.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your Pokédex for as long as the Bot is running, unless you ask us to delete it or we reset or remove it as allowed by our Terms of Service. Temporary logs are kept only as long as needed for security and abuse prevention. If we shut the Bot down, we will delete the stored data.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data, including keeping the Bot's token and stored data out of any public code. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, and to have it deleted. You can also object to or ask us to restrict how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below, including your Discord user ID so we can find your data. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## 11. Children

The Bot is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you think a child has used the Bot in breach of Discord's rules, contact us and we will delete their data.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Continuing to use the Bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## 13. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute, except that we may seek urgent relief in any court that has jurisdiction over you.
